Welcome

One school. Connected workspaces.

Afaq brings school records and daily work into one system. Each person signs in to a workspace shaped around their responsibilities.

Who uses Afaq?
  • Administrator: oversees school setup, records and approvals.
  • Finance Officer: follows invoices, payments, fees and expenses.
  • Teacher: manages assigned classes and records learning.
  • Parent: follows linked children and school communication.
  • Learner: accesses courses and personal learning records.
How to use this handbook

Start with the shared navigation guide, then follow the chapter for your role. The screen labels in bold match the app.

What you can see depends on your account, school setup and assigned permissions. Some pages may be empty until the school publishes or records information.

Sign in, then follow the page trail.

Open Afaq in the app or on the school website and sign in with the account details issued to you.

Move between pages

  1. Use the left menu on a wide screen.
  2. On a phone, use the bottom tabs or tap More to open the menu.
  3. Use Back to return to the previous page and Home to return to your role overview.
  4. Use Refresh if a page needs to reload.

Find learning content

Learners can search subjects and courses inside My courses. Record pages may also have their own search and filters, such as invoices or students.

The workspace menu opens pages directly. It does not search for dashboard pages.

If sign-in does not work

1
Check the account details.
Use the school-issued phone number or email and password. Check for extra spaces.
2
Check the connection.
The app must reach the school's Afaq service. Reconnect to the internet or the configured school network, then retry.
3
Ask the school office.
Staff accounts and parent-child links are managed by the school. Public learner registration is only available if the school has enabled it.

Protect your account. Do not share a password or leave a signed-in account open on a shared device. Sign out from the account menu when you finish.

Set up the school workspace

Administrator · School records and day-to-day oversight

Begin with the school's core structure. Accurate classes, staff and subjects make later registers and reports easier to use.

Review Overview

›

›
Monitor activity

Students

Open Students to find and maintain student records. Confirm identity and class details before making a correction. Keep a reason when the form requests one.

Classes

Open Classes to review school classes and their assignments. Check the campus, teacher and class details so attendance and academic work are scoped correctly.

Teachers

Open Teachers to manage staff accounts, teaching assignments and workload. Assign the required class and subject before expecting class work to appear.

Subjects and courses

Use Subjects for the school subject catalogue and Courses for online learning content. Published courses and lessons become available through learner access rules.

A sensible setup sequence

1
Confirm school structure. Check campuses or branches, class names and reporting periods where available.
2
Connect people to their work. Verify staff accounts, class assignments and subject ownership.
3
Review access. Confirm that staff see their assigned classes and that families are linked to the correct students.
4
Check the overview. Use real counts and activity to spot missing setup before a new term begins.

Access is scoped. A user's role, school and branch assignments determine which records can be opened. If a record is missing, check the assignment with the office instead of sharing another person's login.

Review, approve and publish

Administrator · Academic oversight

Teaching records move through review steps so families see approved information and school reports reflect their source records.

Teacher records work
›
Teacher submits
›
Administrator reviews
›
Publish for families

Attendance

Use the attendance register and summary to review daily records. Unmarked students remain unmarked; confirm the class and date before assessing coverage.

Assessment Review

Open submitted assessment work, review class and reporting-period scope, then approve or return it according to the school's process.

Reports

Teachers prepare reports from attendance, Qur'an sessions and published assessments. Review the submitted snapshot before publishing it to parent and learner views.

Course access

Review course access requests, inspect linked invoices and notes, and apply the available decision. Payment confirmation may grant access through the configured workflow.

Before publishing a report

  • Confirm the correct class, learner and reporting period.
  • Refresh source records and check attendance, Qur'an and published assessment information.
  • Read teacher feedback and the parent-facing preview for clarity.
  • Publish only when the report is ready to be seen by the family.

Published reports are snapshots. Later source changes do not silently rewrite an already published report. Follow the school's correction process if an issued report needs attention.

Separate roles, separate duties. Qur'an session recording belongs to assigned teachers. Administrators review academic work but should not enter teaching observations on behalf of a teacher.

Home

Review the reported invoice count, collections, outstanding amount and expenses. Use shortcuts to open overdue invoices, pending payments and course access requests.

Invoices

Search, filter by status and move through the register. Open an invoice to check the learner, fee, due date, balance and linked payment history.

Course access

Follow requests for paid courses, check the linked invoice and office notes, and review the request's current status. Access follows the school's configured grant or confirmed-payment flow.

Work a payment question

1
Find the learner's invoice using the register search or status filter.
2
Open invoice details and follow the linked payment record.
3
Check the latest saved payment status and reference. Open the receipt when available.
4
Return to the list; filters remain in place for continued work.

If a save or network request times out, check the register before trying again. An uncertain result could already have been recorded.

The finance workspace records and displays transactions. It does not initiate a charge, edit financial history or reconcile a payment provider.

FINANCE OFFICER
02

Maintain fees and finance records

Finance Officer · Finance tools

Fee structures

Search the rate register, review active or inactive structures, and open a form to record a fee amount and billing cycle. Check the branch or school scope before saving.

Expenses

Record a positive amount, expense date and optional description. Use date filters and record details to review entries already in the register.

Financial reports

Select a year and review reported totals and monthly collections and expenses. Preview or save the available CSV export for further analysis.

Finance inbox

Review finance inquiries, search and filter by status, then update a request to NEW, CONTACTED or CLOSED as appropriate.

Before recording an expense

  1. Check that the expense has not already been entered.
  2. Confirm the amount, date and currency shown for the record.
  3. Add a useful description when it helps identify the purpose.
  4. Save once, then confirm the entry appears in the register.

A fee structure is a rate record. Saving it does not automatically create invoices. An expense record documents a cost; it does not transfer money from a bank.

Read financial totals carefully

  • Summary totals reflect what the API reports for the selected period.
  • Invoiced minus collected is a period difference, not necessarily today's debtor balance.
  • Individual entries display their recorded currency. Do not add mixed currencies without conversion.
  • CSV exports contain financial information. Choose an appropriate folder and recipient.

Maintain an audit trail. Use the official register and school procedures to investigate discrepancies. Do not treat an inquiry status as proof that a message was sent or a payment was received.

01

Teach from your assigned workspace

Teacher · Class-based workflow

Start from your Dashboard or My Classes. Class context follows you into the records used for that class.

Dashboard
›
My Classes
›
Select a class
›
Record or follow up

Dashboard and My Classes

Review today's teaching work, assigned classes and timetable. Open a class to reach its students and daily class tasks.

My Students

Search your assigned roster and open a learner profile. Use support notes for relevant follow-up; only access learners assigned to you.

Attendance

Select the class and date, then record each student's actual status. Unmarked students stay unmarked. Correct an entry through the available audited correction flow.

Qur'an Tracking

Select a class and learner, then record the specific reading, memorisation or revision session and observation. These are distinct activities.

Assessments

Choose the class and reporting period, record scores and feedback, review the draft, then submit it for administrator review.

Reports

Prepare a learner report from the selected class and period. Refresh source records, review the feedback and submit the draft for administrator publication.

Keep the class context visible. If you change classes, confirm the new class before creating or reviewing records. Choosing a learner also narrows the work to that learner.

Recording is not publishing. An assessment or report draft becomes visible to families only after it passes the school's review and publication process.

Teachers cannot enrol students, change staff assignments or edit the approved subject catalogue. Contact the school administrator if setup is missing.

Parent · Family workspace

Use the Dashboard for an overview, then open your child's records for detail. On pages that support more than one child, select the child before reviewing information.

Dashboard
›

›
Open a record
›

My Children

Review linked child profiles, class information, attendance, academic progress and recent activity.

Attendance

Check recorded days, notes and teacher remarks. Contact the school if a date or absence note needs clarification.

Qur'an and academic progress

Follow teacher-recorded Qur'an sessions and published subject results. Teacher comments appear with the relevant learning record.

Reports

Open and download reports after the school publishes them. Drafts awaiting review are not shown as final reports.

Payments

Review your child's invoices and confirmed payment records. Open a record to inspect the current details or receipt when available.

Messages and updates

Read published school notices and contact your child's assigned teacher through the private conversation area.

Ask the right question

  • Missing child or wrong class: contact the school office to check the family link or enrolment.
  • Attendance or learning record: message the assigned teacher with the relevant date or subject.
  • Invoice or payment: contact the finance office and include the invoice or payment reference.
  • Report not available: ask whether the reporting period has been reviewed and published.

Privacy follows your family link. The parent workspace shows records for children linked to your account. The school office manages those links.

Courses, lessons and media

Keep the lesson in one learning space.

The course reader keeps lesson notes, resources and playback together so learners can move through a published curriculum.

My courses
›
Course curriculum
›
Lesson reader
›
Course contents

Video and audio

Choose an available recording or load the lesson sample. Playback stays inside the lesson. Use play, pause, seeking and speed controls when available.

Documents and images

Select a resource in the lesson to read a PDF, view an image or open text. Return to the lesson notes when you are ready.

Choose another topic

Open Course contents, expand a module and select an available topic. The reader loads fresh lesson content and releases the previous media.

Adjust reading

Use the text-size controls in the notes or your device accessibility settings. Return to the curriculum when you want to review the course sequence.

A few playback tips

  • Real lesson media is checked against your course access before it opens.
  • Playback does not automatically start or mark a lesson complete.
  • A sample video or audio is practice material, not the teacher's lesson recording.
  • If playback fails, retry or contact the school with the course and lesson title.

Resume means continue with an available unfinished lesson. It does not restore an exact video timestamp. Use the player timeline to seek within a recording during the current session.

Teacher workflow around online courses

Teachers open My courses to work with their assigned online courses. School curriculum assignments and learner access are managed by the appropriate school role.

GOOD PRACTICE
Everyday use

Keep work clear and secure.

Before you save

  • Check the selected learner, class and date.
  • Review amounts, status and reporting period.
  • Read the confirmation before submitting a form.
  • Wait for a saved result before leaving the page.

When a page looks empty

  • Refresh the page once and check the connection.
  • Review the selected class, child, year or status filter.
  • Ask whether the school has assigned or published the record.
  • Use the page's retry action if a request failed.

Protect school information

  • Use your own account and keep your password private.
  • Open only the records needed for your role.
  • Sign out on shared devices.
  • Store downloaded reports and finance exports carefully.

Ask for support

  • Contact the school office for account, family-link or class-assignment issues.
  • Contact the finance office for invoices and payment records.
  • Contact the assigned teacher about attendance or learning records.
  • Include a page name and record reference; never send your password.

Common status labels

Draft

Saved for preparation; not yet final.

Under review

Waiting for the responsible reviewer.

Published / Confirmed

Available or recorded as the status indicates.

Need help? Contact the school using its official support route. Afaq does not display staff passwords or ask you to share them in a support request.
